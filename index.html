Who is St. Gonsalo Garcia?

Born in Vasai, St. Gonsalo Garcia became the first Indian saint of the Catholic Church. He served as a missionary in Japan and was martyred for his faith in 1597.

His life symbolizes courage, sacrifice and devotion for the Christian community of Vasai.

Historical Timeline

  • 1557 – Born in Vasai
  • Missionary work in Japan
  • 1597 – Martyrdom at Nagasaki
  • 1862 – Canonized as Saint
